# AI Applications — ITAI 2372

**Houston Community College · Prof. Sridhar Ganti · Summer 2026**

Cross-industry study of applied AI: four in-depth case studies with original solution proposals, a group fraud-detection project, and six industry/topic reports. All written work is fully cited (MLA 9) and delivered as formatted, professional documents.

## Case Studies

| Case Study | Description |
|---|---|
| [AI in Government](./Case-Studies/AI_in_Government_CaseStudy_Medina.docx) | UK HMRC Connect fraud detection (£4.6B recovered in 2024/25) and the London ULEZ camera-vandalism backlash · proposed a Proactive Benefits Enrollment System redirecting the same data-matching infrastructure toward £24B in unclaimed UK benefits · illustrated throughout with the video game *Orwell: Keeping an Eye on You* |
| [AI in Manufacturing](./Case-Studies/AI_in_Manufacturing_CaseStudy_Medina.docx) | Siemens' Amberg plant (99.9990% quality standard) via a 2026 *Information Systems Journal* field study, contrasted with Tesla's 2018 "excessive automation" admission · proposed an 8D-Aligned Explainable AI Quality Assistant targeting the documented worker-trust gap |
| [AGI in Transportation & Logistics](./Case-Studies/AGI_in_Transportation_CaseStudy_Medina.docx) | Narrow AI's long-tail edge-case problem in autonomous vehicles vs. DeepMind's Levels-of-AGI framework · proposed an AGI-Orchestrated Logistics System reasoning across trucking, warehousing, and supply chains |
| [Ethical & Societal Implications of AI](./Case-Studies/A09_Ethical_Societal_Implications_Medina.docx) | Facial recognition and predictive policing in law enforcement, analyzed through real wrongful-arrest cases (ProPublica's COMPAS investigation, Detroit's 96% error-rate admission) and the Ubisoft *Watch Dogs* series as an extended narrative lens |
